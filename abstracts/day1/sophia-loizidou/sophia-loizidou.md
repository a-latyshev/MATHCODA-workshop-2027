# Arctan-skewing: a new skewing scheme on the hypertorus

In this talk we propose and study arctan-skewing, a general approach to skew any
symmetric distribution on the unit hypertorus. Arctan-skewing retains the main
advantages of the popular sine-skewing mechanism, but improves on it by
overcoming its two major limitations. These are a condition on the skewness
parameter and a singular Fisher Information matrix in the vicinity of symmetry
when combined with well-known symmetric models from the literature such as the
circular von Mises or bivariate Cosine models. Arctan-skewing thus represents a
compelling alternative to model skewed data on the circle and torus, notably
from the field of protein bioinformatics. We present its stochastic properties
and a maximum likelihood estimation procedure, and devise optimal tests for
symmetry against our new class of skewed alternatives. Finally we will show that
arctan-skewed models better fit protein data compared to their sine-skewed
counterparts.

Joint work with Christophe Ley (University of Luxembourg, Luxembourg).
