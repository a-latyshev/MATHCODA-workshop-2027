Subject: MATHCODA Workshop, 25–28 January 2027, Belval

Dear colleagues,

I’m pleased to share details of the MATHCODA Workshop, organised by our Doctoral Training Unit in Mathematical Tools for Complex Data Structures and funded by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR), taking place on 25–28 January 2027 in MSA 3.370, Maison du Savoir, Campus Belval.

We have an excellent line-up of international speakers across four thematic days:

- 25 January - Statistical Inference and Modelling: including Andrea Meilan (Universidad Carlos III de Madrid), Rosaria Simone (Naples Federico II), Sophia Loizidou (Luxembourg) and John Kent (Leeds).
- 26 January - Economics and Stochastic Processes: including Chiara Amorino (Universitat Pompeu Fabra) and Victor Chernozhukov (MIT).
- 27 January - Probability: including Lucia Celli (Luxembourg) and Bruno Loureiro (ENS).
- 28 January - Uncertainty in Mechanics: including Bojana Rosić (TU Wien) and Johann Guilleminot (Duke).

The mechanics day may be of particular interest to our department, as may the broader themes of statistical modelling and stochastic processes. Talk titles and abstracts are still being collected; the developing programme is available on the workshop website:

https://a-latyshev.github.io/MATHCODA-workshop-2027/

Places are limited. If you’d like to join us, please contact Luís da Maia at luis.maia@uni.lu, indicating which days you can attend. We ask that participants commit to attending at least two full days. We will confirm places in early January, taking into account demand and the number of places available.

I hope to see many of you there.

Best regards,
