Subject: MATHCODA Workshop, 25–28 January 2027, Belval

Dear colleagues,

I’m pleased to share details of the MATHCODA Workshop, organised by our Doctoral Training Unit in Mathematical Tools for Complex Data Structures and funded by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR), taking place on 25–28 January 2027 in MSA 3.370, Maison du Savoir, Campus Belval.

We have an excellent line-up of international speakers across four thematic days:

- 25 January - Statistical Inference and Modelling: including John Kent (Leeds), Dimitris Karlis (Athens University of Economics and Business) and Robert Gaunt (Manchester).
- 26 January - Economics and Stochastic Processes: including Victor Chernozhukov and Isaiah Andrews (MIT), and Mathieu Rosenbaum (Université Paris Dauphine–PSL).
- 27 January - Probability: including Bruno Loureiro (ENS), Stefano Favaro (Turin) and Mihai Nica (Guelph).
- 28 January - Uncertainty in Mechanics: including Johann Guilleminot (Duke) and Bojana Rosić (TU Wien).

The mechanics day may be of particular interest to our department, as may the broader themes of statistical modelling and stochastic processes. Talk titles and abstracts are still being collected; the developing programme is available on the workshop website:

https://a-latyshev.github.io/MATHCODA-workshop-2027/

Places are limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis. We ask that participants commit to attending at least two full days. If you’d like to join us, please contact Luís da Maia at luis.maia@uni.lu, indicating which days you can attend.

I hope to see many of you there.

Best regards,
